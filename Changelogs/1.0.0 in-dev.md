## v1.0.0-zeta:
- ADDED: Emotion Limit Break item is now functional.
- CHANGED: Emotion levels now work a bit differently to make sense of the Emotion Limit Break item: 
	- Battle Units (Enemies/librarians) now have a higher technical cap of emotion levels (30 levels)
		- The actual cap for librarians is decided by the total count of Emotion Limit Break Items the player has
		- The actual cap for enemies is decided by the vanilla chapter of the reception (so 1 for Rats & 7 for Oliver and Ensemble)
	 - Instead of getting abno pages of only a certain level on team emotion level up, player can get any abno page with level less or equal to team emotion level (so you get access to level 3 pages at emotion level 3 instead of 5, and can get level 3 pages at any point after level 3 too)
	 - ^ That DOES mean that you can get all 15 abno pages in a single battle
	 - You get an EGO page at every level starting with 3rd (meaning you'll have every EGO page the floor has at level 7)
	 - The amount of emotion coins needed to level up is adjusted for levels past 5 with 13 being the cap for levels 15+ (technical limitation that i COULD remove but don't want to)
	 - The amount of light gotten for emotion is adjusted:
		 - You get 1 light for every levels 1-5
		 - You get 1 light for every second level for levels 5-15 (so at levels 7, 9, 11, 13, 15)
		 - You get 1 light for every third level for levels 15-30 (so at levels 18, 21, 24, 27, 30)
	 - You get a speed die every 4th level (so at levels 4, 8, 12, etc.)
	 - UI is changed a bit so the game doesn't break at levels past 5 and when a unit has more than 5 abno pages (Yes, you CAN stack up to 15 abno pages on a single unit now. Go nuts.)
	 - Also, when receiving the emotion limit break item mid-battle, the max emotion level will be updated!
- CHANGED: Connect window now has more exception handling messages with additional info on where the exception occured (so i can figure out the issue faster)

- ADDED: Built-in AP Client inside the game that can be opened/closed by pressing Tab:
	- Archipelago tab contains chat window which shows every received AP message, and which you can use to chat with other players/use commands from the game.
	- Hints tab shows all the known hints related to your game. You can also hint from this window. (This tab updates every time you open it, so let me know if it lags with a lot of hints!)
	- Settings tab contains client-sided settings that you can change and .yaml's settings that you can override on the client-side. You can only override settings that won't require game regeneration/break the game in any way. Some settings may require you to reconnect.
	- Debug tab contains some info that is not normally seen but is useful for debugging.

- ADDED: New "Layered" progression mode (Old is now named "Battle Graph") (Thanks Yar):
	- Instead of opening stages by completing previous stages and finding required books, they're opened in bulk (layers) by burning vanilla books (they don't give any drops!).
	- Vanilla chapters become actual part of the logic: you can't open layers of the next chapter unless you complete a preconfigured percent (50% by default) of the stages in current chapter.
	- Book of Everything is back for this mode exclusively: The drops are now placed in different "bundles" ("BoE chapters") based on the number of each bundle and estimated game progression. Burning BoE gives max amount of drops in that bundle. There is a limited amount of bundles per chapter, meaning that if logic places more BoEs in a chapter than there is bundles, you won't be able to burn all BoEs until you unlock next chapter.
	- Booster Packs are also present in this mode but nerfed to 4 drops per pack!
	- IMPORTANT: Old "Battle Graph" Mode is supposed to get removed next update, but if you actually liked it and think it can be improved, PLEASE send feedback.
